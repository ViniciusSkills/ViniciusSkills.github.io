The Greatest Movies in Cinema History

Movies have the power to inspire, entertain and change the way we see the world. IMDb's Top 10 list celebrates some of the most highly rated films in cinema history, selected through ratings and reviews from movie fans around the globe.

From powerful prison dramas to legendary crime stories and unforgettable superhero adventures, these films have earned their place among the greatest cinematic achievements of all time.

Number 1 · IMDb Top Rated

The Shawshank Redemption (1994)

IMDb Rating: 9.3/10

Director: Frank Darabont

Starring: Tim Robbins, Morgan Freeman and Bob Gunton.

The Shawshank Redemption tells the story of Andy Dufresne, a successful banker who is sentenced to life imprisonment for a crime he claims he did not commit.

Inside Shawshank State Penitentiary, Andy develops an extraordinary friendship with fellow prisoner Ellis "Red" Redding. Through hope, determination and friendship, he finds ways to survive the harsh realities of prison life.

Genre: Drama
Runtime: 142 minutes
Release: September 1994

Number 2 · IMDb Top Rated

The Godfather (1972)

IMDb Rating: 9.2/10

Director: Francis Ford Coppola

Starring: Marlon Brando, Al Pacino, James Caan and Robert Duvall.

The Godfather follows the powerful Corleone crime family, led by patriarch Don Vito Corleone. The story explores family loyalty, power, betrayal and the criminal underworld of organised crime in America.

When an attempt is made on Vito's life, his youngest son Michael becomes increasingly involved in the family business. His transformation from an outsider into a powerful mafia leader is at the heart of this unforgettable film.

Genre: Crime, Drama
Runtime: 175 minutes
Release: March 1972

Number 3 · IMDb Top Rated

The Dark Knight (2008)

IMDb Rating: 9.1/10

Director: Christopher Nolan

Starring: Christian Bale, Heath Ledger, Aaron Eckhart and Gary Oldman.

Set in Gotham City, The Dark Knight follows Batman as he attempts to protect the city from a dangerous criminal known as the Joker.

The Joker creates chaos throughout Gotham, challenging Batman's moral principles and pushing the city to its limits. As the conflict intensifies, Batman must face difficult decisions about justice, sacrifice and the difference between good and evil.

Heath Ledger's unforgettable performance as the Joker helped make this one of the most celebrated superhero films ever created.

Genre: Action, Crime, Drama
Runtime: 152 minutes
Release: July 2008

Number 4 · IMDb Top Rated

The Godfather Part II (1974)

IMDb Rating: 9.0/10

Director: Francis Ford Coppola

Starring: Al Pacino, Robert De Niro, Robert Duvall and Diane Keaton.

The Godfather Part II continues the story of the Corleone family, exploring Michael Corleone's efforts to expand and protect his criminal empire.

The film also takes audiences back to the early life of Vito Corleone, showing his journey from a young immigrant in New York to a powerful crime boss.

Through two parallel timelines, the film explores ambition, family, loyalty and the consequences of power. Robert De Niro's performance as young Vito adds another unforgettable dimension to the Corleone family story.

Genre: Crime, Drama
Runtime: 202 minutes
Release: December 1974
